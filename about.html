About House of Gianna

A fashion brand where design decisions and supply chain decisions are made together.

Our story

House of Gianna was founded by Onosetale Winifred Areghan, an entrepreneur whose background spans law, business administration and global supply chain management. Working in procurement, supplier management and logistics across Canada, the UK and Nigeria showed her how much of a garment's quality is decided long before it reaches the customer.

That experience shapes the brand. Every piece is designed with its fabric, its makers and its delivery in mind.

What we value

Quality first

Fabric and workmanship are checked at every stage of production, not only at the end.

Transparency

We keep clear records of where our materials come from and who makes each piece.

Reliable partners

We build long-term relationships with suppliers and are clear about expectations on both sides
